# Panchatantra & Indian-Fable Story Bank

A curated shortlist of public-domain Indian fables to adapt into Mode B
(general narrative) packs. Each is bloodless / child-safe in the **adaptation
notes** given — the originals sometimes end harshly, so the softening is
deliberate and required.

To turn any row into a full package, copy the structure of
`content/the_elephants_and_the_mice/STORY_PACKAGE.md` (8 pages, character sheets,
locked style block, per-scene prompts, review checklist, cast manifest).

## House rules for every adaptation
- **Mode B fields:** `mode: "general"`, `template: "narrative"`, `lowArousal: false`, no coping cards.
- **Length:** 8 pages, one clear sentence each, read-aloud friendly (short words, gentle rhythm).
- **Tone:** warm, a little playful; the moral lands in the final 1–2 pages, never preachy.
- **Safety:** no death, blood, weapons, hunters/people on-screen, or genuine menace; antagonists are *outsmarted*, not hurt.
- **Style:** reuse the **same locked style block** (soft 2D anime, warm brown outlines, Indian forest/temple/village settings) so the whole library looks like one world.
- **Cast:** one-off characters per story; list each as a `castId` and gate via `cast_manifest.json`.
- **learningGoal:** write it parent-facing — it powers the "what your child practised" summary.

## Suggested launch set
A balanced first shelf for the paid library:
- **Mode A (social stories):** "When the World Gets Loud" *(built)* + 2 more (e.g. a new place, sharing/turn-taking).
- **Mode B (fables):** "The Elephants and the Mice" *(built)* + "The Foolish Lion and the Clever Rabbit" + "The Talkative Tortoise."

Six finished stories (3 + 3) is enough to make the free-sample → paywall funnel
feel worth paying for.
